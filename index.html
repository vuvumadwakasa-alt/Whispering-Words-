How to Use Whispering Words Studio

A quick guide for writers and filmmakers. Your current version stores drafts in this browser on this device.

1. Start a film

Choose Create New Film, give the project a title, then open Screenplay Studio. Keep a separate project for each film.

2. Write your screenplay

Tap the white page and type with your phone keyboard. Use INT., EXT., CHARACTER, DIALOGUE and DUAL DIALOGUE tools to insert screenplay elements.

3. Import safely

Import UTF-8 plain-text .TXT or .Fountain. If your script is in Word or PDF, open it in that app and export/copy it to plain-text .TXT first. Never import a .docx or PDF as if it were text.

4. Save and back up

Use Save and Export .TXT regularly. Browser storage can be cleared and does not automatically sync to another phone or account.

5. Develop the film

Use Story Intelligence to shape premise and structure, Character Studio for character notes, World & Locations for setting notes, and Director/Production/Post sections to plan your workflow.

6. Sibali

The current Sibali chat is a prototype and is not connected to live AI or the internet. Verify industry rules, movie availability, books and grant deadlines with official sources.

Safety & Trust for Creatives

Safety guidance for meeting people through film and creative work. This page is guidance, not a substitute for a real reporting and moderation system.

Before meeting someone

  • Keep first meetings in a busy public place or an established film/arts venue during daytime.
  • Tell a trusted person where you are going, who you are meeting and when you expect to return; arrange a check-in.
  • Verify their identity and work through independent references, previous credits or a video call.
  • Do not send money for a casting fee, travel promise, job placement or grant guarantee without independent verification.
  • Do not share your home address, ID/passport, banking details, passwords, private images or live location with strangers.
  • For auditions, ask for the role, production name, location, contact details and written terms. Leave if pressured or uncomfortable.

For future community features

Before public launch, implement report/block, moderation queues, verified contact channels, privacy controls, age-appropriate protections, abuse response procedures and a clear privacy policy. Emergency danger: move to safety and contact local emergency services or a trusted person.

Important: the current Community is a prototype, not a live stranger-matching service. No real reporting, verification or moderation backend is connected.

Account & Login — Production Requirements

This standalone HTML file cannot provide secure multi-user login by itself. We will not pretend a local-only password form is real account security.

Required before public launch

  • Real authentication provider and server-side session handling over HTTPS.
  • Verified email or phone, password reset, sign-out and session expiry.
  • Private per-user project storage with server-side access rules.
  • Account deletion, privacy notice, consent and data-retention policy.
  • Rate limiting, secure password handling, backup and recovery tests.

Until connected, drafts are stored locally on this device only. Anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to access them. Do not store sensitive personal information here.
